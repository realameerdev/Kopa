# Kopa

> **Run your business by simply talking to it.**

Kopa is an AI-powered business operating system designed for small and growing African businesses.

Instead of forcing business owners to understand complicated accounting software, spreadsheets, dashboards, and business terminology, Kopa allows them to interact with their business naturally.

A business owner can simply say:

> “Sold 3 black shirts for ₦45,000.”

Kopa understands the statement, structures the information, records the transaction, updates the business data, and uses that information to provide useful business intelligence.

Kopa is designed around a simple idea:

**Business owners should be able to talk to their business, not become accountants just to manage it.**

---

## The Problem

Millions of small businesses across Africa operate without reliable business records.

Many business owners rely on:

- Memory
- WhatsApp conversations
- Paper notebooks
- Spreadsheets
- Calculator apps
- Bank statements
- Informal records

This makes it difficult to answer basic questions such as:

- How much did I make this month?
- What did I spend?
- Which products sell the most?
- Which products actually make money?
- Who still owes me money?
- How much inventory do I have?
- How has my business performed over time?
- What should I pay attention to?

Traditional business software can solve some of these problems, but often requires users to understand accounting concepts and manually enter structured information.

Kopa approaches the problem differently.

---

# The Solution

Kopa turns everyday business activity into structured business intelligence.

Instead of navigating complicated forms for every transaction, users can communicate with Kopa naturally.

For example:

> “Sold 3 black shirts for ₦45,000.”

Kopa can interpret this as:

- Product: Black Shirt
- Quantity: 3
- Revenue: ₦45,000
- Transaction type: Sale
- Date: Current date

If the cost of the shirts is known, Kopa can also calculate the applicable profit.

For example:

> Selling price: ₦45,000  
> Product cost: ₦27,000  
> Gross profit: ₦18,000

If a delivery expense of ₦5,000 is recorded:

> Estimated net profit: ₦13,000

If the product cost is unknown, Kopa does not invent it.

Instead, it tells the user that the cost is unavailable and can ask the user to provide it.

This principle is central to Kopa:

**Unknown information remains unknown until the business provides it.**

# Financial Accuracy

Kopa is designed around an important financial principle:

**Revenue is not automatically profit.**

For example:

A business sells products worth ₦100,000.

That does not necessarily mean the business made ₦100,000 in profit.

If the products cost ₦65,000:

> Revenue = ₦100,000  
> Cost = ₦65,000  
> Gross Profit = ₦35,000

If additional business expenses total ₦10,000:

> Estimated Net Profit = ₦25,000

Kopa therefore follows several rules:

1. Revenue is not automatically treated as profit.
2. Profit calculations require relevant cost information.
3. Missing information remains unknown.
4. Kopa asks for missing information when it is necessary.
5. AI responses should be grounded in actual business records.
6. Recorded figures are distinguished from estimates.
7. Kopa does not fabricate financial information.

This is important because an AI business assistant should not create financial certainty where the underlying data does not support it.

# Business Passport

One of Kopa's long-term concepts is the **Business Passport**.

The Business Passport turns a business's actual activity into a structured business profile.

Instead of relying on a business owner to manually prove everything about their business, Kopa can build a record based on activity captured through the platform.

A Business Passport may contain information such as:

- Business name
- Business category
- Recorded revenue
- Number of recorded transactions
- Customer activity
- Product activity
- Top products
- Business activity history
- Transaction timeline
- Other verified business activity recorded by Kopa

The Business Passport is not designed as an arbitrary credit score.

It is intended to become a structured representation of business activity.

With appropriate user consent and privacy controls, this type of business data could eventually help businesses interact with:

- Suppliers
- Financial institutions
- Insurers
- Fintech platforms
- Business service providers
- Other commercial partners

The long-term vision is to create a useful digital identity layer for businesses that traditionally operate with limited formal records.

# Data Privacy & Security

Kopa is designed with data ownership and security in mind.

Important principles include:

- Users can only access their own business data.
- Authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication.
- Firestore security rules protect business records.
- External OAuth credentials should be handled through secure server-side configuration.
- Client applications should never expose private client secrets.
- API secrets should never be committed to the public repository.
- Connected account tokens should not be treated as ordinary client-side data.
- Business data should only be accessed according to the user's authorization.

Kopa's public repository and documentation intentionally do not expose private credentials, API secrets, OAuth secrets, or other sensitive configuration.

# Responsible AI

Kopa's AI layer is designed to assist businesses without pretending to know information that was never provided.

The system should:

- Use actual business data when answering questions.
- Clearly distinguish recorded data from estimates.
- Ask for missing information when required.
- Avoid inventing costs, sales, customers, or financial figures.
- Avoid presenting uncertain information as fact.
- Preserve the user's control over business records.
- Require appropriate confirmation for important actions.

AI should make business management easier, not make business records less reliable.
